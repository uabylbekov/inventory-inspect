# Snapshots Business & Monetization Plan

## 1. Executive Summary
**Snapshots** is a high-end B2B utility designed for property managers, real estate agencies, and individual landlords. It automates the property inventory and inspection process, providing legal-grade evidence through timestamped photo comparisons and professional PDF reporting.

The core business value is **Risk Mitigation** (reducing deposit disputes) and **Operational Efficiency** (automated diff reports).

---

## 2. Target Audience
1.  **Professional Property Managers**: Managing 5–500 properties who need a centralized team workflow.
2.  **Short-Term Rental Hosts (Airbnb/VRBO)**: Requiring fast, "native-feeling" check-in/check-out proofs.
3.  **Real Estate Agencies**: Providing premium, branded reports to property owners to increase their service value.

---

## 3. Monetization Model: "B2B Growth-Gated SaaS"
The app uses a three-tier model that scales with the size of the user's property portfolio.

### Price Points
*   **Standard (Free)**: $0 (For evaluation and single-unit landlords).
*   **PRO (Professional)**: **$49.99 / month** (Perfect for small teams).
*   **ENTERPRISE**: **$199.99 / month** (Designed for large agencies).

---

## 4. Tiered Feature Matrix

| Feature | **Standard (Free)** | **PRO (Professional)** | **ENTERPRISE** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Properties** | Max 1 Unit | Up to 10 Units | **Unlimited** |
| **Managers** | 1 User Only | Up to 5 Managers | **Unlimited** |
| **Photo Attachments** | ❌ Text-only | ✅ Included | ✅ Included |
| **PDF Branding** | Snapshots Logo | Custom Company Logo | **Full White-label** |

---

## 5. Strategic Monetization Hooks

1.  **Unit Scaling**: The jump from 1 to 2 properties is the primary conversion moment.
2.  **Manager Seats**: As a team grows, the need for shared synchronization makes the app a mission-critical tool.
3.  **Professional Image**: The ability to send reports with a proprietary agency logo is a core requirement for commercial clients.
